### `CD-02` — Use of tx.origin for Authorization  *(Severity: Medium · Status: Unresolved)*

The contract uses tx.origin to determine rules in the _transfer function. This is a security risk as it can be exploited by malicious contracts to bypass authorization checks.

**Recommendation:** Use msg.sender instead of tx.origin for all authorization and rule-based logic.


### `CD-01` — Arithmetic Precision Loss  *(Severity: Low · Status: Unresolved)*

The _transfer function performs division before multiplication, which can lead to minor rounding errors in fee calculations.

**Recommendation:** Refactor arithmetic to perform multiplication before division to maintain precision.
