## Audit Summary

The DERC20 contract is a standard ERC-20 token with additional governance and vesting capabilities. The protocol relies on a centralized owner to manage inflation rates and pool access. While the code follows standard OpenZeppelin patterns, the high degree of administrative control poses a significant centralization risk to token holders.

> **Final Recommendation:** To improve security, consider transitioning the ownership to a multi-signature wallet or a decentralized governance contract (DAO) to mitigate the risk of a single point of failure. Implementing a timelock on all privileged functions would provide transparency and allow the community to react to administrative changes.

### `CP-01` — Centralized Minting Authority  *(Severity: Medium · Status: Unresolved)*

The owner can call updateMintRate and release to increase the total supply of the token. This allows the owner to dilute the holdings of all existing token holders at their discretion.

**Recommendation:** Implement a hard cap on the total supply or move the minting authority to a decentralized governance process with a mandatory timelock.


### `CP-05` — Centralized Transfer Control  *(Severity: Medium · Status: Unresolved)*

The owner can use lockPool and unlockPool to enable or disable transfers for specific pools. This gives the owner the ability to effectively freeze user funds or restrict liquidity at any time.

**Recommendation:** Remove the ability for the owner to arbitrarily halt transfers, or restrict this functionality to a decentralized emergency pause mechanism.
