## Audit Summary

The DERC20 contract is a standard ERC-20 token with additional governance and vesting capabilities. The protocol relies on a centralized owner to manage inflation rates and pool access. While the code follows standard OpenZeppelin patterns, the high degree of administrative control presents a significant centralization risk to token holders.

> **Final Recommendation:** To improve security, consider transitioning the owner role to a multi-signature wallet or a decentralized DAO governance contract to mitigate the risk of a single point of failure. Additionally, implement a timelock mechanism for all privileged functions to provide transparency and a window for users to react to administrative changes.

## Security Findings

### `CP-01` — Centralized Minting Authority  *(Severity: Medium · Status: Unresolved)*

The owner has the authority to mint new tokens via the updateMintRate and release functions. This allows the owner to increase the total supply at will, which can dilute the value of existing holdings.

**Recommendation:** Restrict minting capabilities to a pre-defined schedule or move control to a decentralized governance body with a timelock.


### `CP-05` — Centralized Transfer Control  *(Severity: Medium · Status: Unresolved)*

The owner can use the lockPool and unlockPool functions to enable or disable token transfers. This gives the owner the power to effectively freeze user assets or prevent trading at any time.

**Recommendation:** Remove the ability to pause transfers or implement a community-voted pause mechanism to prevent potential abuse by the owner.
